# joint-collaboration

Use for the default Sol × Opus research mode in HuaweiCup_F_2026. Read PROJECT_RULES.md, 09_handoff/JOINT_CONTEXT.md, PROJECT_STATE.md and 02_analysis/consensus/JOINT_COLLABORATION_PROTOCOL.md before dispatching a round.

Save each contribution before passing it on: Sol draft → Opus extension marked KEEP/MODIFY/ADD/QUESTION/REJECT → Sol synthesis with reasons for accepted and deferred ideas → Opus review focused on Critical defects → itemized consensus. If Critical defects remain, repeat correction and review. Do not treat two answers as a vote or invent a compromise between incompatible mathematics.

Keep JOINT_WORK_LOG.md short and record only research changes, open questions, experiment needs, convergence and the next owner. Refresh JOINT_CONTEXT.md from confirmed evidence and current contributions; keep SOL_CONTEXT.md and OPUS_CONTEXT.md task-specific.

Codex may preserve, route, implement and test a CONFIRMED specification. When the models disagree materially, record the issue and evidence needed in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md or DEBATE_XXX.md and return it to them. Independent analysis is an exceptional tool with a recorded trigger, not the default first step. Stop at the current handoff unless a follow-up authorizes the next research round.
